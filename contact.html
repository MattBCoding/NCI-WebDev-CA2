Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I buy a single day pass to try the gym out?
A:Yes! Student day passes are €12 (or €15 standard) and give you full access to the gym floor, changing rooms, and Fuel Bar. If you sign up for a monthly membership within 48 hours, we'll take the day pass cost straight off your first month.
Q: Do I need to bring my own padlock or towel?
A:No padlock needed—all lockers use built-in digital PIN codes. Sweat towels are complimentary for all members, and shower towels are free on the Pro & Recovery plan (or €2 to rent).
Q: Is there parking nearby?
A:While most of our members walk, cycle, or take the Luas, paid underground parking is available 3 minutes away at the IFSC / National College of Ireland car park on Mayor Street.
Q: How do I partner with QUAYFORM for an NCI society event?
A:Use the inquiry form above and mention your society name and rough headcount—we love hosting charity spinathons, run club collabs, and study-week wellness mornings.
