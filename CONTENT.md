### Membership FAQ (Accordion)

* **Q: Do I need to be an NCI student to get student pricing?** *(Expanded by default)*
**A:** Not at all! While we’re right on NCI’s doorstep, any full-time third-level student with a valid Student Leap Card or college ID (NCI, Trinity, DCU, TU Dublin, UCD, etc.) qualifies for our student rates. Non-student Docklands residents and professionals can join for €10 extra per tier.
* **Q: Is there a 12-month contract or cancellation fee?**
**A:** Never. Every membership is a rolling 30-day plan. You can cancel or freeze your membership for up to 3 months over summer or study breaks directly in the QUAYFORM app with 14 days' notice.
* **Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan mid-semester?**
**A:** Yes—switch tiers whenever your schedule changes, and the new rate kicks in on your next billing date.
* **Q: What happens if I miss a booked class?**
**A:** We ask for at least 4 hours' notice if you need to cancel a class so another member on the waitlist can grab the spot.

### Contact FAQ (Accordion)

* **Q: Can I buy a single day pass to try the gym out?** *(Expanded by default)*
**A:** Yes! Student day passes are €12 (or €15 standard) and give you full access to the gym floor, changing rooms, and Fuel Bar. If you sign up for a monthly membership within 48 hours, we’ll take the day pass cost straight off your first month.
* **Q: Do I need to bring my own padlock or towel?**
**A:** No padlock needed—all lockers use built-in digital PIN codes. Sweat towels are complimentary for all members, and shower towels are free on the Pro & Recovery plan (or €2 to rent).
* **Q: Is there parking nearby?**
**A:** While most of our members walk, cycle, or take the Luas, paid underground parking is available 3 minutes away at the IFSC / National College of Ireland car park on Mayor Street.
* **Q: How do I partner with QUAYFORM for an NCI society event?**
**A:** Use the inquiry form above and mention your society name and rough headcount—we love hosting charity spinathons, run club collabs, and study-week wellness mornings.
